### Problem Statement

First of all, let me explain the background and problem statement.  

I think many people drive a car every day and driving makes our daily lives more convenient and enjoyable.  
However, when a problem occurs with a car, to solve the problem quickly is important to keep continue driving safely and comfortably.  

One major challenge is that we often do not understand the meaning of a warning light like the one shown in this image.  
More specifically   
 - Some warning lights require the driver to stop the vehicle immediately in a safe place.  
 - Other warning lights require inspection by a Toyota dealer.

In general, one of the most difficult things is to determine what action to take rather than only understand the meaning.  
In this project, To address this problem, I developed an Agentic RAG based on the official owner’s manual to answer the meaning of the warning light and how to take action.  
As a limitation, This project covers only the 2021 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid Owner’s Manual.  

---

### Agent Architecture

Let me talk about the Agent Architecture.  
In this project, I built three approaches.  
  - The first one is the LLM-only Baseline.  
    - In this approach, we generate the answer from LLM-Only which have only on its pre-trained knowledge.  
  - The second one is Basic RAG.  
    - In this approach, in addition to baseline-LLM, RAG searches the official owner’s manual before generating answer.  
  - The third one is Agentic RAG.  
    - In this approach, in addition to Basic RAG, I added three steps CLARIFY, REVISE, and ESCALATE for agentic process.  
    - In the CLARIFY step, we clarify the important missing information in user's question.  
    - In the REVISE step, we revise the generated answer to improve the accuracy.   
    - In the ESCALATE step, we escalate question for the human review if it's necessary.

### Tools and Data Sources

In terms of "Tools and data sources", I will briefly explain the details which I commonly used for the three approaches.

For the AI model, I ran Qwen2.5 for three approaches in common locally.  
For the knowledge source, I downloaded "2021 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid Owner’s Manual.PDF" and referred Page 642-651 from basic RAG and Agentic RAG.  
For the embedding model for both basic and agentic RAG, I used sentence-transformers.  
And For the retrieval process for both basic and agentic RAG, I used Facebook AI similarity search to retrieve the records related to the user's question based on vector similarity.

### Evaluation Methodology

Let me explain "Evaluation methodology".   
  
I measured Accuracy and Latency as the evaluation metrics.  
 - Accuracy is the average of "meaning coverage and action coverage"
   - Meaning Coverage is, how the generated answer covers the expected meaning from the question.  
   - Action Coverage is, how the generated answer covers the required action.
 - Latency is the average response time of each system.

About the Test Data Set, the test set contains 12 questions.

### Evaluation Results

Let me explain the results.  

This table shows the evaluation results of the three approaches based on the 12 test questions I showed earlier.  

In terms of accuracy, Agentic RAG achieved the highest accuracy of 0.955.
On the other hand, In terms of latency, Agentic RAG had the longest average latency of 7.078 seconds.

# Conclusions and Future Improvements:

In conclusion, 
In this project, I built an Agentic RAG for warning-light questions about the 2021 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid and compared three approaches, LLM-only baseline, Improved RAG, and Agentic RAG.  

In terms of Accuracy, Agentic RAG achieved the highest accuracy.  
This means, CLARIFY, REVISE, and ESCALATE processes contributed to improve the quality of the generated answer.  

I think that this system is useful for providing initial information about warning lights.  
However, this system cannot replace an actual inspection or diagnosis by a qualified Toyota dealer because the knowledge and supported questions are limited.  

For future improvements, I'd like to support a wider range of questions, improve the reliability of the answers, and reduce the latency.
